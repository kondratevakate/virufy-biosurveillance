    CHECK IN · 15 MINUTES

    Check in at a public place

    Your phone listens for up to 15 minutes and counts coughs around you. Audio is not recorded or stored. Only the cough count, the listening time, the kind of place and, if you set one, a 1 km map cell would be shared. In this prototype they stay on this device. You can stop at any time.

    EVIDENCE

    How it works and what we measured

    How it works

    1. The phone runs a cough detector (YAMNet audio classifier, MediaPipe) in the browser.
    2. Only cough counts per 15 minutes, the kind of place and a map cell of about 1 km would leave the device. In this prototype they stay on it.
    3. Zones with fewer than 10 participants are hidden.
    4. An outbreak is defined as a sustained rise of the cough rate above the zone's own baseline, after accounting for how many people are listening. Each zone runs a bank of Poisson CUSUMs (tuned for rises from 5% to 100%) against its weekday baseline, with the alarm level set by simulation for about one false alarm in 180 days.
    5. Aircraft wastewater series are the second signal; weeks above their recent baseline are marked.
